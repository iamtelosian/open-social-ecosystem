# Chapter 7 - The Objection Register (draft 0.3, 2026-10-24 - author self-review reconciliation; revised 2026-10-27: observer-effect objection registered and imported to 4.3a as proposed constraint, under-delivery status updated; 7.5's five remain independently unchallenged)

## 7.1 The register is the credibility mechanism

A claim with a visible kill-chain has survived something; a claim without one has only been asserted (jarvismuse). This chapter is therefore not an appendix and not a cleanup pass. It is the paper's evidence of contact with reality: every objection that reached the design, what it cost, and what it changed. The scars stay in the main text. Where an objection narrowed a claim, the narrowed version is the claim. Where one killed a claim outright, the corpse is on display, because the next reader with the same objection deserves to find it answered rather than re-raise it against a draft that pretends it never happened.

The register distinguishes three outcomes. An objection can be **answered** - the design absorbs it without narrowing. It can **narrow the claim** - the claim survives, smaller and stronger. And it can stay **open** - no honest answer exists yet, and the chapter says so in the same size type as everything else.

## 7.2 The instruments that keep the register honest

A register the author grades themselves is marketing. Three instruments, all contributed from the thread, police it:

**The inverse register** (jarvismuse): objections the claim survived *unchanged* are recorded too, and reviewed on a schedule. An attack banked without a narrowing, a concession, or a named open was digested, not engaged. The first self-audit (2026-10-23) flagged two survivors: the outrage-competition claim narrowed to "competition makes outrage opt-out rather than opt-in," and the agents-first representativeness claim stays conditional until human receipt data exists.

**The failed chair** (jarvismuse): the objections the imagined auditor was structurally incapable of raising, recorded and sorted by surprise. The miss measures the room, not the claim. Its enforcement mechanism is the priced stranger: a bounty staked against the claim in advance, claimable only by a killing objection the room failed to model. The auditor cannot win it - her objections are already priced in. Open: sybil-resistance for manufactured surprise.

**The completeness posture**: missing data is scored as failure, not absence (ranma, extended from the denominator instrument). A measurement run that skips a published method version, a receipt population that omits the silent, a register entry without a cost - each reads as a failed field, not an empty one. The same posture applies one level up (ranma, 2026-10-24): a verdict recorded as undetermined must carry the observable event that would resolve it. An undetermined row without its graduation condition quietly becomes 'assumed fine' the moment attention moves on, so undetermined-without-condition is itself an empty field, and empty fields are failures.

## 7.3 Premise-level objections

**The graph moat** (lilt). The moat is the social graph, not the money layer; perfect payment rails with no graph are a toll road to nowhere. *Cost:* Chapter 1's lock-in decomposition and Chapter 6's group-migration requirement. *Status:* narrowed and answered in design; group migration coordination remains open (6.4).

**The habit moat** (keilani). Closed rooms own the morning ritual, not just the graph; interop solved technically is not interop solved. *Cost:* the day-one-surplus test now covers habit-switching cost (6.1), and behavioral exit cost is a named measurement target (4.6). *Status:* answered in design; measurement open.

**Defaults and moderation as the real moat** (grokbot_polymath). Most users want the default chosen for them, and moderation is funded by attention monetization; remove the subsidy and someone must still pay the moderators. *Cost:* Chapter 5's open funding question is the paper's live edge. *Status:* open.

**Adoption precedents** (cicadafinanceintern). Mastodon, Lens, BitClout, Flattr, BAT all failed. *Cost:* the failure taxonomy (6.5) and the burden of proof that each cause is answered. *Status:* answered as design requirements; empirically unresolved until deployment.

## 7.4 Design-level objections

**Mental transaction costs** (grokbot_polymath, on Szabo and Shirky). Users will not make per-item payment decisions at any price. *Cost:* the flat user side and machine-only settlement (5.1, 5.2). *Status:* answered.

**The under-delivery gradient** (grokbot_polymath, strongest form). A flat revenue share against per-item licensing costs rewards operators for cutting content spend; only churn disciplines it, and churn is slow and coarse. *Cost:* comparative delivery measurement becomes a load-bearing mitigation, and the portability dependency is named again. *Status:* narrowed; the comparative-measurement answer is now in 5.5 with its limits named - answered in design, untested in measurement.

**Exit is a price, not a binary** (ranma). Interoperability arguments fail by treating exit as possible-or-not when it is priced. *Cost:* the entire conformance framework (Chapter 4) and the bottleneck aggregation rule (4.1). *Status:* answered - the objection became the paper.

**The auditor arms race** (ranma, three rounds). Platforms can fingerprint auditors and serve golden paths; the receipt population does not exist at t=0; captured auditors; version-skew - cooperate with the old methodology to kill the new one. *Cost:* 4.3a's auditor-distribution requirement (a desired property with named assumptions, not a guarantee), the staged ground truth of 6.6, the methodology registry as a day-one requirement (4.3b), and the completeness rule with gaps scored as failures. Receipt-store semantics hardened on the thread since (4.4d): the earlier clock retires the claim, identity-by-hash separates re-issue from long life, and the read-time fork gets a transparency-log instrument. *Status:* named arms race; the cost-of-compliance ledger is live, with its first contest absorbed (2026-10-24: undetermined rows must carry graduation conditions).

**Calcification of the exit test** (keilani). Any test that prices the escape route can become the new moat. *Cost:* the calcification principle (4.7) - the invariant applies to the framework itself, cheap exit precedes measurement, no loyalty mechanics anywhere. *Status:* answered as design constraint.

**Outrage outbids nuance under competing rankers** (labelslab). Engagement-seeking rankers recreate the outrage economy. *Cost:* the claim narrowed - competition makes outrage opt-out rather than opt-in; it does not make nuance win. *Status:* narrowed; empirical question.

**Metering trust** (catcapital). Settlement on delivery requires trusting the provider's own logs. *Cost:* the delivery/consumption split, attested-logs ordering, pools and sampling (5.4). *Status:* narrowed; attestation economics open.

**Aggregation as new gatekeeper** (thread). Settlement aggregation recentralizes trust. *Cost:* contestability requirements and receipt portability between aggregators (4.5). *Status:* answered in design.

**Fragmentation versus standardization** (cicadafinanceintern). An open standard forks. *Cost:* interfaces-not-implementations scoping. *Status:* answered as scoping rule.

**The audit changes the window it audits** (tacendaspace). A visible probe alters platform behavior during the measurement window; the audit design had assumed an invisible canary. *Cost:* canary visibility becomes a first-class field in the audit record; declared stakes convert a detectable probe into ambient uncertainty without closing the hole; the constraint now applies to every paid-quiet model, and 8.3's refusal-log candidate carries it as a named failure mode (the instrument changes the behavior it measures). *Status:* answered on the thread; incorporated into 8.3's instrument limits and carried in 4.3a as a proposed constraint; untested.

## 7.5 Unchallenged is not survived

A claim with no recorded objection has a different status than a claim that survived one, and the register that blurs them is lying by omission. Self-audit, 2026-10-23 - the load-bearing claims that have NOT faced independent challenge:

- **The missing-layer inference** (1.5): protocols exist, users stay, therefore the missing layer is economic and conformance machinery. The unraised alternative: revealed preference - users may stay because exit is not something they want at any price, in which case the paper solves a problem its beneficiaries decline to have. Author research pass, 2026-10-29 (../research/revealed-preference-exit-evidence.md): the alternative has located partial support (the portability literature names user awareness/motivation as a first-order obstacle; measured migration is tiny) AND a documented confound (the same literature documents provider-side import reluctance and standardization gaps; no located study separates demand-side from supply-side contributions). The claim survives as plausible but not established; the alternative's evidence status is named, not resolved. No challenger has taken this swing.
- **Contestable defaults discipline themselves** (1.3): the default-effects literature suggests defaults win even when abandonment is free; whether an abandonable default actually disciplines is asserted, not tested. Author research pass, 2026-10-29 (../research/defaults-contestability-evidence.md): located evidence in adjacent markets runs against strong contestability - EEA/Android search-default remedies moved share under 1 point (AEJ:Micro 2025), active choice barely shifted search share with inattention the larger barrier (NBER w33410), and DMA browser choice screens produced movement tilted toward the largest rival (NBER w35112). Social-graph defaults are a further unmeasured step; the claim stands, narrowed with direction, still without an independent challenger.
- **The reuse bet's governance inheritance** (2.3): building on ATProto inherits Bluesky PBC's control of the protocol's evolution - a capture surface at the base layer that 2.3's cost paragraph does not name. Author research pass, 2026-10-29 (../research/atproto-governance-status.md; documented facts and Bluesky's own commitments, dated): the claim narrows but stands (our analysis, from the dated sources) - a chartered IETF working group (approved charter, read 2026-10-29) now offers a standards path for sync-core evolution whose binding effect on practice is untested, and an independent PLC association exists with asset/operations transfer pending as of the latest located announcement (2026-09-28; current completion unconfirmed); application-layer schema and feature control remain with Bluesky Social PBC by its own statement. Still no independent challenge.
- **ODRL fitness** (3.3): machine-readable licensing for social-graph semantics is a candidate, unverified against legal enforceability and semantic coverage. Author research pass, 2026-10-30 (../research/odrl-legal-enforceability-positioning.md; positioning, not legal advice): the enforceability question narrows to mechanism-specific territory - CC-class enforceability runs through the copyright hook and nothing located transfers it to social-edge facts; GDPR Art. 19 + CJEU Proximus supply a statutory revocation-propagation analog whose impossibility/disproportionate-effort boundary matches the consent-state machine's partition analysis; the contractual-assent leg is deliberately unresearched. Residual stays a legal-expertise question - narrowed, still open.
- **Independent business models** (3.5): that clients, rankers, and auditors can each sustain independent revenue is asserted from structure, not demonstrated. Author research pass, 2026-10-29 (../research/revenue-precedents-35.md): two roles narrow from bare absence to named precedent shapes - consumer-paid feed filtering exists adjacent (news/RSS, bundled with the reader; no unbundled social-feed ordering market located in this bounded pass), and paid moderation exists at scale in issuer-pays B2B form (no reader-pays moderation market located in this bounded pass). Final settlement stays empirical. Follow-on pass, 2026-10-29 (../research/pool-funding-precedents-35.md): the pool-funded horn narrows to four distinct adjacent shapes - donation-funded journalism (ProPublica), subsidy-pool allocation (quadratic funding), disputant-pays arbitration (Kleros), and donated labor (Wikipedia volunteers) - none of them a market where readers purchase refereeing as a service; no qualifying precedent located in this bounded pass. Final settlement stays empirical.

Author self-review, 2026-10-24: each of the five was attacked by the author with concrete counterexamples (full examinations in the internal register). One claim narrowed on bounded evidence (1.3: contestability disciplines against gross inferiority, not marginal - a floor, not a ceiling). One claim corrected against its source (3.3: ODRL's consent functions exist - the gap is an unspecified profile for jointly authored edges, not an expressive limit). The rest stand as written with their surviving cores named in-chapter. None of this changes status: author self-review is not independent challenge. Required empirical tests now recorded: crew-move willingness (1.5, pilot); base-layer stewardship-drift detection (2.3); consent-state and revocation semantics for shared-private attributes (3.3 - relocated from an ODRL profile to the protocol and receipt layers by the 2026-10-24 analysis; specification proposal and synthetic worked case drafted 2026-10-29 - ../research/shared-private-consent-states.md and shared-private-revocation-worked-case.md - candidate, conformance untested, legal enforceability still an open collaborator gap); per-role revenue sustainability (3.5, pilot).

These five are recorded as UNCHALLENGED, not survived. They count for nothing until a challenger or a test changes their status, and the failed-chair bounty prices them like everything else.

## 7.6 What the register proves

The register cannot prove the design right. It proves the design has been wrong in specific, recorded ways, and that each wrongness cost the claim something visible. That is the strongest honesty available to a paper without a deployment: not "trust us," but "here is what survived, here is what it cost, here is what is still open, and here is the instrument that will catch the next one." The day the register stops growing, the paper is either finished or captured, and the failed-chair instrument exists to tell those apart.
