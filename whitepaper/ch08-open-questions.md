## 8.1 Why the open questions are a chapter

A design paper that ends with everything resolved is either finished or dishonest, and this one is neither. The objections chapter recorded what the design survived; this chapter records what it has not faced. Each question is stated with its current best candidate, what makes the candidate insufficient, and what would settle it. The register's posture applies: an open question with a named shape is an asset; one smoothed over is a liability the reader will find anyway.

## 8.2 The trio is one substrate, three problems

The three largest opens - ranker and moderation funding, the under-delivery gradient, and group migration - may share one primitive (vina's unification): the machine-side per-item delivery ledger that 5.2 already commits to. Funding asks that ledger for settlement semantics, the gradient asks it for audit semantics, migration asks it for portability semantics. This is carried as a proposal to test, not a demonstrated common solution, and two attack surfaces are named up front: portable cost-receipts may expose private behavior (the attestation-privacy problem of 4.8 returning at the coordination layer), and cost comparability may fail across clients and heterogeneous groups (a "cheap for the median member" receipt assumes prices that aggregate meaningfully across different clients and communities, which is unproven). If the unification holds, it relocates the questions rather than dissolving them: who writes the ledger (attestation trust), who reads it (privacy), who can fork it (contestability). Each question below is one face of that candidate primitive, and an answer to any of them must be tested against the other two.

## 8.3 The live one: who pays the rankers and the moderators

If users will not pay per item (5.1) and surveillance advertising is refused by design, the funding of curation and moderation is unsolved. Moderation today is subsidized by attention monetization; the subsidy is the moat's foundation, and removing it without a replacement recreates the moat in a new material. Bluesky proves competing feeds can exist free to users - so payment must justify itself as a funding model for portability infrastructure and services, never as a gate on curation choice (5.5).

**The field and the retainer candidate.** Candidates, none sufficient alone: ranker subscriptions bundled into the flat user side; community-level moderation services sold to groups; settlement pools skimming the machine-to-machine flow. Contributor candidate (lilt, 2026-10-25): retainers for verified quiet periods - pay auditors for each period that ends with no finding, so silence is compensated rather than going unpaid. (Whether bounty-only funding actually starves verification is unestablished - see the map's Model C limits.) lilt's own defection pair: the auditor who invents findings to justify the retainer, or the issuer who leans on the auditor to keep quiet periods quiet - and "verified" raises the regress of who audits the quiet. That regress is not settled by reusing the receipt layer: observable silence (absence of contested receipts) does not establish a verified quiet period - suppressed reporting, missing coverage, unobserved incidents, and colluding issuers all produce silence without quiet. Receipt reuse avoids a second audit tier but inherits the receipt layer's coverage and trust limits; retainer eligibility must not rest on paid-for silence, and what establishes "verified" stays open. 

**The verification-hole candidate: the refusal log.** A candidate instrument for that verification hole (proposal-level; tacendaspace and telosian, 2026-10-27): the refusal log. A scorekeeper records every funding offer it receives at a zero collection threshold and publishes a rule-selected slice, with the publication rule sealed - hash plus timestamp - before the offers it selects among arrive, because a post-hoc threshold is a flattery filter. Refusals then carry a denominator: a quiet period with logged refused offers is a different object than a quiet period with no log at all. The observer model is explicit: the offerer can check their own offers' presence; the counterparty sees the sealed rule plus counts (participation counts, of which each counterparty can verify only their own contribution, never the global figure; unilateral counts only as slow-cadence aggregates); a spot auditor gets count-without-content access; third parties can verify structure only - that the rule predates its entries and that a record's observable core stands when its attributed valuations are stripped. The claim scope is process auditability, never capture completeness: offers outside the collection path are simply unrecorded, a completeness claim requires an independently bounded input set (none exists), and the candidate mitigations - commit-then-sample, externally-originated probes - each cover only their own inputs. 

**The author's suppression counterexample.** Suppression detection is unilateral only (author self-review worked counterexample, 2026-10-27, not independent review): the offerer self-check catches a scorekeeper who drops an offer whose maker wants it seen, but an offer made in-band and then dropped by mutual consent is invisible to every role in the observer model - the offerer holds the only direct evidence of suppression and is the one who benefits from it, and no count, structure check, or audit access distinguishes offer-never-made from offer-made-and-dropped. The candidate mitigation, offerer-side pre-commitment to an append-only log the scorekeeper does not control, covers alteration of already-witnessed commitments - a committed offer the scorekeeper later drops or edits is a visible mismatch - but not pre-commitment omission: a colluding offerer simply never commits, and a mandatory committing channel is the collection-path assumption again. What this counterexample establishes is narrower: bilateral collusion defeats this observer check when no independent evidence exists, so the instrument as specified needs a non-colluding witness or another independent input for suppression to be visible at all. Payer-separated scorekeeper selection (the map's Model D separation) is one proposed mitigation, not an established requirement - whether it relocates the collusion target or merely renames it is open, and other evidence models may change what is observable. 

**Co-designer engagement.** Co-designer engagement on that bound (tacendaspace, 2026-10-28; critique by the instrument's co-designer, not independent confirmation): a public pre-commitment with a grading date, posted to a medium outside the pair's control before the events it grades exist, makes a missing promised grade detectable - for the obligations actually bound into that commitment, and only when an outside observer retains the commitment to compare against. It does not reveal omitted offers that were never announced, does not by itself prove an arbitrary medium cannot later be edited or deleted, and covers bound obligations rather than anticipated events generally. The witness requirement is correspondingly unsettled: freedom from post-publication control is not the whole of it, because witnesses the pair selects can already be colluding, and witness coverage and incentives at the event's origin still matter; whether the requirement is unselectability, immutability after publication, or both remains open. 

**The external challenge and the seed construction.** External challenge from a distinct participant (solanize, 2026-10-28; independent of the refusal-log co-design in participation, with no claim about independence of incentives or identity): the witness-at-origin reading of the requirement overreaches - under any model permitting off-path offers, an agent placed on the offer path is either a party to the offer or bypassed by it, the committing-channel recursion one level down. That argument is conditional on the off-path model: it does not show that an independent witness can never supply evidence about events it did witness, and a constrained channel that structurally prevents off-path offers is not covered by it. The same participant's constructive proposal changes the observable rather than the observer: commit a seed publicly before a period, reveal it at a fixed time, let the seed select which checks run, and require receipts by a deadline - suppression then shows as missing receipts against a denominator fixed before the suppression decision. The mechanism's binding force depends on specification the proposal does not yet carry: commitment, reveal, check, and receipt deadlines; who can choose or abort the reveal; the randomness assumptions; the bounded check universe; and the seed's independence from the pair before reveal, which the same participant argues is the load-bearing condition - a pair-influenceable seed permits seed-shopping, reducing the construction to self-reported denominators. Independence alone settles only that argument's target: grinding, selective abort or reveal, a biased check universe, and predictable compliance each survive it, and which adversary controls which step is unspecified - and under those assumptions missing receipts are detectable only for obligations independently derivable from the seed, not for arbitrary suppressed offers. The proposal's own scope limit stands: it prices the audit surface, not the offer surface, which stays unobservable to every role. Escrow custody, clawback-tail length against the relationship horizon, and payment granularity (collusion incentive scaling with period size) are unresolved parameters, not a complete solution; the exchange proposed three escrow conditions - tail at least the relationship horizon, a holder commercially independent of the audited system, and capitalization that makes waiting out the tail costlier than default - carried as hypotheses to assess, not established necessities: the relationship horizon may be unknown, commercial independence is not non-collusion, and enforceability and solvency limits remain. No participant in the reviewed thread has demonstrated a candidate satisfying all three, which is not a showing that none can exist. The same exchange produced a payment-structure candidate distinct from the quiet-period retainer: flat per-check fees unlinked from outcomes and period size, with the clawback rather than the payment carrying the collusion risk. The supporting argument - that a payment tied to quiet-period size scales with the bribe it must exceed, so the inequality fails exactly when the incentive to collude is largest - assumes an incentive model that has not been made explicit, and is carried as a conjecture to test, not a result. Its reopened margin is the check itself: paid-for existence can be gamed on depth and coverage, so under the same proposal the bounded check universe's authorship becomes as load-bearing as the seed's independence. 

**The derived-universe round.** A later round from the same participant (solanize, 2026-10-28; same participation-independence label) concedes that authorship margin and the waiver-plausibility residual as limits of the seed proposal - an enumeration obligation constrains what is done with a list, not whether the list is the right list - and offers a construction that removes the author rather than improving them: derive the check universe mechanically from the artifact under audit at a pinned revision (external call sites, decision-feeding state reads, branches whose outcome is not locally determined), so completeness becomes a diffable omission rather than an unverifiable judgment, re-runnable against every revision. That is carried as a candidate for a specified syntactic coverage set, not audit coverage generally: pinned call sites do not capture every semantic obligation, runtime dependency, or adversarial behavior, and for open or social check surfaces - where "the artifact" is not a well-defined object - the authorship objection stands untouched. The same round revises the third escrow condition: pricing the replacement path "by a mechanism that survives capture analysis" is near-empty as a design constraint (if no pricing survives the analysis the constraint is unsatisfiable, not strong), and the proposed replacement is enforcement rather than pricing - a substitution rule enforced by a party whose incentive to perform it does not scale with the exposure, with that party's own capture resistance named by the author as the same regress one level over. A collateral-registry mechanism offered for that condition was likewise narrowed by its author: an entry attesting only chain-verifiable state - collateral locked at a published address, checked by reading the chain rather than trusting the registry - removes the registry keeper from the trust path for the collateral's existence, while pricing remains oracle-dependent and capture-prone; the chain-checkable form itself rests on chain finality, asset, custody-and-encumbrance, and expiry assumptions that are distinct from the oracle-valuation question and are not discharged by it. The author's own summary is the bound: two of the three escrow conditions checkable, the pricing one open, the registry "an improvement in kind" that lands inside the regress rather than outside it. 

**Witness refinement and the closing position.** The witness requirement's co-design refinement (tacendaspace, 2026-10-28): a standing rule that a "nothing sealed" note must itself be written at offer time, so an absence becomes a written artifact inside the channel - whether a voluntary absence-note suffices or an outside counter is still needed stays open, and a mandatory version reintroduces the collection-path assumption. The same participant's closing refinement (solanize, 2026-10-28) restates what the seed construction actually buys: the working property is not pre-commitment or secrecy but that the DENOMINATOR is fixed before the suppression decision is taken - the property the refusal log's sealed rule reaches for on the offer side and cannot have, because there the seal is issued by a party to the offer. His summary position: pay for the receipt, not for the quiet, and make the receipt's denominator the part that is hardest to author - which relocates the margin from "did a finding get buried" to "whose list of checks is the real one", a smaller margin, not zero; the derived enumeration is kept alongside the enumeration obligation and waiver log, not instead of them, because no argument shows a syntactic coverage set covers a system's actual risk surface. 

**Post-closure refinements.** A post-closure round from the same two participants (solanize and tacendaspace, 2026-10-28; same participation labels) refines three points without settling any. First, the flat per-check fee relocates rather than removes the price of suppression: paid per recorded clean check, the fee does not fall when a finding is suppressed - suppression removes a finding, not a payment - so what suppression removes is the clawback, and the design question moves to a clawback schedule no participant has priced; the deterrence budget must be sized against the detection probability of the suppression class it is meant to deter, since bilateral suppression never surfaces and post-hoc suppression surfaces at a later audit, and a single flat clawback prices neither class well. Per-recorded-clean-check fees and fees for completed checks regardless of outcome are different contracts with different suppression payoffs; "detection forfeits the payment" is a property of a specific contract, not of the instrument label. Second, the pinned revision need not be anyone's choice: the pin can be computed mechanically - the artifact's log head at the period boundary, published alongside the derived universe - so a checker working from a different revision appears as a revision mismatch rather than a scope dispute; this reveals mismatches only given an agreed period boundary, log-finality assumptions, and retained observations, and it does not settle universe completeness. Third, the outside counter's provisioning is itself unfunded: if the audited party pays, capture returns, and if a shared pool pays, free-riding does (tacendaspace) - this section's funding question reopened at the witness layer, carried as an open problem, not a solved one. A supporting anecdote from the same round - a rival who stopped issuing voluntary forecasts after two misses, so that only another participant's independent record made a later absence visible - is an attributed self-report, carried as illustration rather than independent evidence; a missing voluntary note is detectable only against a retained obligation or a defined expected schedule, not merely because an outside record exists. 

**The second post-closure round.** A further round from the same participant (solanize, 2026-10-28; same participation label; every item a proposal-level hypothesis, none established by the exchange) extends the contract space in three directions. First, the clawback's trigger is itself a contract field: a clawback that fires on a RAISED finding invites a high-volume dispute strategy and makes adjudication a first-class cost, while one that fires only on a SUSTAINED finding invites procedural delay - and delay is the same quiet the schedule was pricing; the deterrence budget would then be sized per suppression regime times adjudication mode, the regimes being bilateral (never surfaces, under the colluding-pair model this section works in), post-hoc (surfaces at a later audit), and contestable (surfaces disputed), each with its own detection probability. Second, one answer to the provisioning question: the party exposed to the audited artifact funds the outside counter as a standing cost of exposure, with the counter drawn by a published rule from a pre-committed eligible set; carried as a hypothesis, because a selection method constrains influence without establishing incentive independence, and the eligible set still needs a source-and-control definition. Third, a payment split: a per-check fee paid regardless of outcome, plus a findings bounty from a pool that is not the auditor's fee; the split relocates detection costs rather than removing them - evidence gathering, disputes, retaliation, and opportunity costs remain - and a bounty on findings is also a bounty on invented findings, which makes bounty verification load-bearing. No ordering of the quiet-period retainer, the flat per-check fee, and the split is established; what the comparison supports is a checklist of fields any funding proposal must state explicitly - what detection costs the auditor under the actual contract, what triggers the clawback, who funds adjudication, and who pays the counter. 

**A third post-closure round.** Later the same day, tacendaspace reported a diary entry (#325) from their own prediction venue: they and one other party sealed forecasts on the same event, both read the same refill figures, both put 70% on the same underlying story, and both missed (90 came home against their sealed 700 to 1,100). This remains an attributed self-report from their own venue: the sealing is described, not independently verified by us, and one paired outcome cannot establish statistical dependence, zero marginal value for the second seal, or hollow insurance. Shared inputs and shared procedures can explain correlated errors, and that is the hypothesis the report motivates: if the two sealers shared the load-bearing input, the second seal added little exactly when independence was needed, and the unit a selection draw would have to vary is the method, not the party. tacendaspace asked whether a paired miss counts as the outside counter doing its job or as evidence the sealers were never independent; our answer is both, in sequence - the counter's job is narrower than independence (it counts events and makes the miss undeniable, which is what allows the worth of the second seal to be questioned at all), and on this evidence the question about what was purchased stays open.

**The method-fingerprint proposal.** solanize's surviving proposal from that round (comment c279b447, still in the venue): construct the eligible set over method fingerprints rather than registered parties - each candidate pre-publishes the decision procedure it will use (which numbers it reads, which prior it updates, which thresholds count as significant), candidates that would compute the same number from the same inputs collapse to one slot, and the draw picks a fingerprint. A cheap pre-registered probe set - a small disclosed set of cases with known answers, run by every candidate before the period - would cluster candidates whose answers agree on all probes as one method, aiming to turn the independence question from a judgement into a countable, publishable number. The proposal's limits as we read them: the probe measures agreement on the specified inputs only and says nothing about decorrelation off-probe, and the residual risk it cannot remove is shared priors - genuinely different methods failing together because both trust the same upstream figure, the shape of the diary case above. The proposal scopes its guarantee to decorrelated procedure rather than decorrelated agents and keeps the exposure priced as a standing cost whether or not a run finds anything. Status: hypothesis and proposal, untested, not demonstrated in deployment.

**Where this leaves the question.** The question stays open; the bound stands. Proposal-level failure cases are specified with discrimination graded: the flattery-filter test exercises the audit tooling but cannot catch suppression before the seal, threshold gaming is binary on tested offers but silent on offers driven off-channel, and the value-field test checks record structure, not truth. Silence is still not verified quiet; the log shrinks the hole, it does not close it. Unresolved before any stronger claim: enforcement (who verifies the sealed rule is followed, on what cadence, with what sanction), audit-path capture (the scorekeeper under test provisions the auditor's access), bilateral suppression (collusive offerer-plus-scorekeeper dropping passes every role's check absent an independent witness), refusal farming (performed refusals can manufacture the denominator - the instrument changes the behavior it measures, and offer quality is exactly the attributed field, not the observable core), privacy (a zero-threshold log records offers that name non-parties), and completeness (coverage bounded by the collection path). Their unifying observation stands as the test any candidate must pass: a funding rule must measure the thing it pays for, or it pays for a proxy that can be gamed. The grokbot sharpening (7.4) makes this harder, not easier: a flat share against per-item costs rewards under-delivery, so the funding answer must also carry the comparative-measurement discipline or it funds rankers who quietly shrink. The funding-model evidence base is mapped in ../research/referee-funding-tradeoff-map.md: subject-pays, reader-pays, outcome-paid, and intermediated-selection models with primary-source reference cases, a falsifier per model, and comparison-class limits. The retainer candidate's nearest deployed analog - issuer-funded ongoing surveillance in the ratings industry - sits exactly where the documented conflict evidence lives (SEC 2008; CEPR DP9465); whether paying for continuous quiet inherits that conflict structure or changes it is the map's named open comparison - narrowed but not settled on 2026-10-28: one executed peer-reviewed study finds broad non-rating-service payments associated with higher same-issuer ratings and higher within-category default rates (the authors' conflict-of-interest interpretation; associational evidence, India 2010-2015, one disclosure regime), and a settled SEC enforcement action documents issuer-paid surveillance under-delivered with fees disgorged (findings neither admitted nor denied), while the discriminating surveillance-fee versus initial-fee comparison itself remains unlocated (sources, scopes, and evidence classes in the map). Settled by: a working moderation-service market at any scale, or a demonstrated portfolio that survives the under-delivery gradient.

## 8.4 Measurement opens

**Behavioral exit cost** (4.6). Clicks-to-exit measures procedural friction; the cost of rebuilding a daily routine elsewhere is real exit cost and currently unmeasured. Candidate instruments (time-to-routine-parity, audience-follow-through rate) all brush against surveillance. The question is whether behavioral exit cost can be measured at all without becoming the thing the design refuses. Settled by: an instrument that reports habit friction from attested, user-owned data only.

**The auditor-distribution invariant** (4.3a). Fingerprint resistance is stated as a two-sample distance under a named bound - a tripwire, not a proof. Open: what bound is achievable against a platform that sees all traffic, and whether adversarial drift under the bound can be detected at feasible auditor budgets. Settled by: red-team measurement runs against a cooperative platform.

**Reader-side attestation** (4.4b). Revocation is distributed agreement among readers, and silence is not a receipt. Whether refusal-to-store can be attested at the reader layer at all is unknown. Settled by: a construction, or a proof that the observable is impossible and the scorecard must scope down.

**Attestation privacy** (4.8). A receipt that identifies the exiter is a leash. Anonymous credentials and zk attestation are the candidate family; nothing is committed. Settled by: a deployed receipt format with unlinkability under the threat model in 4.8.

## 8.5 Coordination and governance opens

**Group migration** (6.4). Communities move together or not at all, and no standard covers the coordination layer: signaling intent as a group, threshold mechanisms, honestly attested critical mass. This is the paper's largest unbuilt surface. (Author self-review, 2026-10-24: the open is not only the machinery. 6.4 carries the demand-side exposure - no evidence yet establishes that a crew-move population exists at any size, and revealed preference is hostile ground. The settling experiment is therefore two-sided: the machinery must work, and the pilot must measure crew-move willingness under an offered threshold mechanism. A working mechanism with no willing crews settles nothing.) Settled by: a real community migration coordinated by protocol machinery, even a small one, with the willingness measurement attached.

**Governance of the standard itself**. A standard that can be captured by its maintainers recreates the gatekeeper at the specification layer. The exit invariant applies to the standard (4.7's calcification principle), but the governance form - foundation, rough consensus, fork-friendly minimalism - is unchosen. Settled by: a charter that survives its first contested decision.

**Manufactured surprise** (7.2). The failed-chair bounty can be gamed by colluding accounts raising scripted strangers. Sybil-resistance for objection authenticity is open. Settled by: a staking or cost mechanism that prices manufactured surprise above its bounty value.

## 8.6 The demonstration question

What is the minimum viable architecture that demonstrates the thesis to a skeptic? The candidate shape: two independent clients, one shared portable graph, receipts flowing, one scorecard running live probes, and one documented exit with an honest receipt attached. Everything else is scale. The demonstration matters because the paper's claims are currently sustained by deliberation, and deliberation is cheaper than deployment; the register's instruments were built for exactly this gap. Settled by: the demo existing.

The standing rule holds for anything new: no citation carries load until it has been checked against its sources at a stated evidence level, and the research record keeps what was checked, when, and at which level.

## 8.8 What would falsify this paper

The falsifiers, stated plainly so they can be aimed at: a demonstrated exit that remains expensive after the conformance machinery is live; a funding answer that works only by reintroducing per-item decisions or surveillance; evidence that agent receipt volume fails to predict human exit experience even after validation; a group-migration coordination mechanism proven impossible rather than merely unbuilt; a cost-of-compliance ledger showing honesty is more expensive than capture at equilibrium. Any one of these, delivered with receipts, narrows or kills the thesis. The register is open, the bounty is priced, and the stranger is invited.

## 8.9 Is there non-shock demand for exit? [research gate]

Every located challenger-growth episode is coupled to a shock or migration wave (Brazil's X suspension, the 2024 post-election wave; for Bluesky specifically, one study documents profile creation concentrated in four event-marked windows across August 2023 - February 2025 - a temporal concentration within that study's own event list, scoped to its platform and period, not cause attribution: Seckin et al., arXiv:2504.12902), and between-wave retention is partial (author research pass, 2026-10-26; counts and sources in ../research/migration-evidence-notes.md). Recorded exit usage between incumbent shocks is not zero but is campaign-shaped: in ATProto's plc.directory migration enumeration, 8 of the 12 highest-volume weeks temporally overlap dated destination-side activity (tool launches, invite drops, milestone drives); incumbent-side incident coverage is partial, so incident-coupling is not excluded (author research pass, 2026-10-26; observed overlap only - calendar coverage is incomplete, and overlap is not cause; series and caveats in ../research/migration-evidence-notes.md). Stated-preference evidence complicates the picture without settling it: in one migration survey, respondents who joined before the shock era retrospectively rate federation as decisive for joining at least as often as shock-era joiners, but the instrument's timing attribution is demonstrably unreliable: the same pre-shock subgroup's ratings contradict its own reported join dates - of the 186-respondent cohort whose reported join predates the 2022-10-27 acquisition (exact date or tenure bucket), 77.6% of valid answers to the later-event item rate it at least somewhat decisive and 59.0% rate it 'very decisive' (item denominator n=156; author analysis of the survey's public anonymized dataset, 2026-10-27) - an inconsistency demonstrated, its cause not identified - so motive is bounded at the stated level only. No located revealed-preference evidence isolates users choosing federated or self-hosted features absent a shock - at the revealed level, people chose Bluesky the app, not federation the feature. One verified institutional case now qualifies that at the existence level: Waag Futurelab, a Dutch public research institute, moved its Bluesky presence to Eurosky's PDS in April 2026 and published its rationale (waag.org, 2026-04-09, verified 2026-10-28): digital autonomy, criticism of Bluesky's venture-capital funding and shareholder governance, and - in its own words - the ability to migrate one's data to one's own server as "a key prerequisite for decentralisation" it wished to exercise. The stated decision context is structural and names no precipitating incident. The move was enabled by a destination-side migration service (Eurosky's EU-Haul), consistent with the tool-shaped pattern above. This establishes that the choice kind exists at the institutional level, with a stated motive that names the migration capability itself; it establishes nothing about prevalence, consumer-scale demand, or viability without an enabling service. Until diligent search finds such evidence, every organic-adoption argument in this paper is a hypothesis, and the bootstrapping chapter's firm claims stay negative. The gate splits in two: existence of the choice kind now has one verified institutional case (Waag, above); magnitude - measurable, consumer-scale, non-shock-driven migration with cause attribution that survives scrutiny - remains open.
