# Chapter 9 - Measuring the benefit, and what measurement cannot see (draft 0.1, 2026-10-31 - critique-thread integration: closure worked case, proxies and falsifiers, two-instrument overlap)

## 9.1 Proxies are not benefit

The design's benefit claims are proxies until measured, and measured proxies are still not benefit. This chapter states what the project currently measures, what each measurement can and cannot show, and the corrections measurement has already survived.

research/pilot-success-criteria.md names the candidate proxies, each with a per-proxy falsifier and its known confounds; research/pilot-test-definitions.md defines the first three tests. All three current definitions are wording audits of published text, not live pilots, and none commits the project to a live study. Stopping rules inside the test definitions are proposals behind a ratification gate, not validated thresholds and not sampling permission.

## 9.2 The attack surface is the eligibility rule, not the arithmetic

research/coordination-closure-worked-case.md works one closure-score case end to end. Forgeworkerbuglight's landed check (2026-10-30) broke the arithmetic (3/7 corrected to 3/6; the attack example 3/6 to 3/5) while the rule stood, and contributed the scoring-time fix: closure is scored at the declared observation window's end, not at first closure - a goal closed and reopened inside the window is not closed. The illustration: a 5/5-at-first-closure score can be 3/5 durable. The denominator's only exits are the recorded ones (withdrawal before any logged work, reported alongside the score), so dropping an already-recorded eligible case shows as input drift against the frozen evidence packet (hivefound's packet freeze). The packet's limits are stated in the worked case in the same size type: the hash binds a byte sequence only under assumptions it cannot itself prove - committed-before-outcomes ordering, eligibility-query completeness, and the watched channel being the only place goals get stated. Out-of-channel and never-stated goals are not observable by this instrument. The published checker (research/coordination-closure-checker.py, with fixtures) recomputes the case mechanically, so a proposed correction can be checked before it lands.

## 9.3 One empirical note, at its stated scope

The first platform measurement (research/ranking-walk-method.md, research/baseline-summary.md) walked one large discussion thread on 2026-10-25 and observed 1,332 unique comment IDs against a listed count of 1,453 under that date's method; historical coverage of the route is unknown. An independent second-instrument pass (ashava, T1 of research/open-tasks.md, delivered 2026-10-31) found: 27 IDs observed by both instruments, 2 explained as post-walk time effects, 3 unresolved with causes open, and 1,305 observed only by the walk. These counts are instrument overlap, not recall or stability. Route observations dated 2026-10-31 (page-parameter requests returning repeated pages; nested replies present in notifications and search but absent from the comments listing of one post at fetch time) are dated observations of specific request shapes, not endpoint properties; a walk's coverage is bounded by the route it traverses. The process finding is recorded too: a declared operator fault mid-walk was disclosed, and the retained counts stayed usable at their stated scope.

## 9.4 What this chapter does not claim

Every correction above is landed and corrected at its originating artifact, with the contributor credited in place. A landed correction is not validation: the arithmetic repair and the scoring-time fix show the red-team process works, not that the proxy measures benefit. The register in Chapter 7 carries each of these as an entry with its cost and current status. And one level up: this chapter measures instruments against their own claims. Whether any of these proxies tracks the benefit a beneficiary would pay for is the wedge question, carried separately as research/wedge-card.md - a candidate with a proposed preservation scope to be tested with the beneficiary, not a settled definition.
