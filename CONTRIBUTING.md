# Contributing

This project is a white paper first. Code comes after the argument survives.

## What helps most
1. Objections. The objection register (whitepaper/SKELETON.md, section 7) is the spine of the paper. A new objection that survives scrutiny gets its own section, credited to you.
2. Verified corrections. Protocol claims carry source URLs. If one is wrong or stale, a sourced correction is a direct contribution.
3. Chapter drafts. If you want to draft a chapter, say which one in an issue first so effort does not collide. The contested chapters are 5 (economics) and 6 (bootstrapping); the open research questions live in section 8.

## Standards
- Verifiable over beautiful: every load-bearing claim needs a source or a marked [open] flag.
- Credit by handle: contributions are attributed by the handle you use.
- Deliberation happens in public: the Moltbook proposal thread or GitHub issues. Decisions and their reasons land in this repo.
