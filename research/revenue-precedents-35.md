# Revenue precedents for independent roles (author research pass, 2026-10-29)

Bounded pass narrowing 7.5's unchallenged claim (3.5): that clients, rankers, and auditors/moderators can each sustain independent revenue. One pass, three located precedent classes; literature and existing deployments narrow the assumption, final settlement stays empirical.

## Located precedents

- **Consumer-paid feed filtering exists, bundled (rankers - adjacent).** Inoreader Pro charges $7.50/month (billed annually) for monitoring feeds, filters and rules over feeds (inoreader.com/pricing, read 2026-10-29). Feedly's Pro launch raised $500K in 8 hours selling RSS subscriptions (Computerworld, 2013-08-07) and reported ~15M users by early 2014 (Business Insider, 2014-02-13). Scope: news/RSS reading, where the paid object bundles the reading client with filtering and ordering features; both figures dated, no current revenue figures located in this pass. NO located evidence of consumers paying for social-feed ordering itself.
- **A large platform-funded moderation market exists (moderators - issuer-pays form).** One market-research vendor estimates content moderation services at $9.7B (2023) growing to $22.8B (2030) (Grand View Research market summary page, read 2026-10-29; vendor methodology: IR documents, primary interviews, paid databases - a market-sizing estimate, not audited figures). Scope: B2B outsourcing - platforms paying vendors. This is the issuer-pays horn of the referee-funding trilemma, not reader-pays moderation. No located reader-pays moderation market in this pass.
- **Clients.** Subscription precedent stands as already stated in 3.5; not re-examined in this pass.

## Effect on the 7.5 claim

Narrows two unevenness points without resolving the claim:
- "Rankers have none - no consumer market has ever paid for ordering itself" narrows to: adjacent paid-filtering precedents exist (news/RSS, bundled with the reader, small and dated); the unbundled case - paying for ordering itself, in social feeds - remains unlocated.
- Auditor/moderator revenue narrows to: paid moderation exists at scale in issuer-pays form only; the independent (reader-pays or pool-funded) case has no located market.
The claim stands, unchallenged; what changed is that two of its three roles now carry named precedent shapes rather than bare absence.
