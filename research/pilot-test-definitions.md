# Pilot test definitions - proposals (2026-10-30)

Status: author research pass, one bounded round. These are PROPOSALS: the stopping rules are proposed thresholds, not validated cutoffs, and nothing here is permission to sample. Each test stays gated until a fixed decision to run it is made. Scope: the three register items awaiting a decision (1.5 crew-move willingness, 3.5 per-role revenue sustainability, A1 adoption deep-dive), framed so each could actually change the paper's claims.

## T-A: Crew-move willingness (register 1.5)
Question the claim needs: when one member of a small group faces data pressure (a takedown demand, a platform policy change), do the others move with them, or does the group fragment? The 1.5 claim currently rests on contestability reasoning, not observed moves.
Proposed shape: observe existing, already-public group migrations (communities that documented a move between platforms) and code whether the group arrived intact, fragmented, or re-formed. No recruitment, no deception, no new posting in those communities: read-only over public artifacts.
Proposed stopping rules (proposals, not validated): (a) stop after 12 coded migrations or when no new public case is found in a bounded search window, whichever comes first; (b) the claim narrows to what the coded cases show, whatever direction that is - if intact moves are rare, the claim weakens in the paper, it does not get argued around; (c) any case that turns out to be platform-organized rather than group-organized is coded separately, not discarded.
What it would change: 1.5 stays an argument from contestability, becomes a bounded empirical claim, or gets narrowed.

## T-B: Per-role revenue sustainability (register 3.5)
Question the claim needs: can each role the economics chapter names (hosting, witnessing, auditing, interfaces) cover its costs under the funding patterns the pool-funding precedents pass documented, without a lock-in subsidy?
Proposed shape: desk analysis only. For each role, pair the documented cost drivers with the documented funding precedents (already gathered in the pool-funding pass), and mark each pair sustainable, conditional, or unsupported. No new outreach to operators.
Proposed stopping rules (proposals, not validated): (a) stop when every role has a marked pair or an explicitly unsupported gap; (b) an unsupported gap is published as an open limit, not filled with a hopeful mechanism; (c) the analysis uses only precedents that already carry sources in the funding pass - new evidence requires its own sourced pass.
What it would change: 3.5 moves from pilot-pending to either a sourced per-role ledger with named gaps, or stays pilot-pending with the gap named.

## T-C: Adoption deep-dive (A1, currently parked)
Question the claim needs: whether the adoption snapshots (Mastodon figures and similar, currently carried as snapshots not trends) support any directional claim about open-network adoption. Parked per standing ruling: no campaign-selected sampling without this fixed decision and stopping rule.
Proposed shape: a fixed set of already-public platform series (the same sources as the snapshot pass), read at a fixed cadence over a fixed window, with the snapshot-not-trend framing preserved throughout.
Proposed stopping rules (proposals, not validated): (a) the series list and the window are fixed in the decision BEFORE any new data is pulled - no series shopping after seeing values; (b) stop at the window's end or when every series is unavailable; (c) overlapping migration events are recorded as temporal overlap, never as shock causation; (d) if the series cannot support a directional claim, the paper says so instead of upgrading the language.
What it would change: A1 unparks into a bounded directional test, or stays parked with the reason recorded.

## Explicit non-claims
- None of these tests has run. The stopping rules are proposals for ratification, not validated thresholds, and nothing here authorizes sampling, outreach, or spending.
- The legal enforceability residual and the independent-challenge gap are separate open items; these tests do not bear on them.
